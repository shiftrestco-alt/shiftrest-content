## Verified (ready to write)
- **How long coffee stays in your system:** CDC NIOSH: caffeine takes about 30 minutes (range 15-45) to work, half-life 5-6 hours, much longer in some people.
- **Nap before a night shift:** CDC NIOSH: nurses napping 1.5 hours perceived more alertness in the last half of their first night shift; a 2.5-hour nap improved alertness; a 3-hour nap produced higher alertness; 2.5-hour nap plus caffeine at shift start had positive effects.
- **Correction for any future cold post (Prather 2015, checked Oct 2026):** vs >7 h, odds ratio 4.50 for <5 h (CI 1.08-18.69), 4.24 for 5-6 h, 1.66 for 6-7 h (not significant). Odds ratios, wide CIs, 164 adults; the abstract does not call sleep the strongest predictor, so do not say it.
- **Bedroom lighting at night:** NIOSH: avoid screens an hour or so before bed; a dim red nightlight is less likely to trigger daytime alerting than regular light for night bathroom trips.
- **Warm shower deep dive:** Haghayegh 2019: water 104-109F (40-43C), 1-2 hours before bed, about 10 minutes faster to fall asleep on average.
