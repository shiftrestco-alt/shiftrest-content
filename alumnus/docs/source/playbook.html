Your homepage already follows Pocket's section order. What made Pocket feel better was the execution: a white canvas, big rounded product media, video everywhere, and an offer engine around the buy box.

Bundle math (example numbers)

With a $139 price and the theme's default tiers: Duo 2 × $139 × 0.90 = $250.20 ($125.10 each); Study group 3 × $139 × 0.85 = $354.45 ($118.15 each). If your landed cost per unit (product + shipping + fees) were $45, the Duo would keep $160.20 of margin against $94 for a single unit. Swap in your real landed cost before raising the percentages.

Guardrails (read before launch)

TopicWhat to do
Fake reviews and testimonialsThe FTC's rule (16 CFR Part 465) bans reviews and testimonials that claim to be from people who don't exist or who didn't use the product, including AI-generated ones, with civil penalties for knowing violations [14]. So: AI actors are fine for demo footage, never as "customers". The video carousel ships with a "Demo footage" note; review sections are for real reviews only.
Sale pricesA compare-at ("was") price must be a real former price (FTC Guides Against Deceptive Pricing). Bundle savings are always truthful because they are measured against your own single-unit price.
UrgencyCountdowns use your real sale end date and disappear when it passes. Low-stock messages use real inventory.
Shipping timesState realistic shipping windows and email customers about delays (FTC Mail, Internet or Telephone Order Rule).
Recording consentSome US states require everyone on a call to consent. The FAQ says this; keep it.
AI adsTikTok requires a label on ads with realistic AI-generated people, voices or scenes (reported live from July 2026) [15]. Label AI ads and keep them away from real private individuals.
Email and SMSUse the consent checkbox wording in the popup; SMS needs express written consent (TCPA).

This is practical guidance, not legal advice. When in doubt, ask a lawyer.

Part 4

Higgsfield prompt pack

One art direction, product accuracy first. Every ID matches a labelled placeholder in the theme.

Workflow

  1. Collect references. Get 3–5 clean photos of the real device (front, back, edge, on a phone) and the cable. Your supplier photos work if you crop out their text.
  2. Make stills first. For product-only shots use Nano Banana Pro with your reference photos attached; it is the best at keeping exact geometry and handling edits. For scenes with people use Soul 2.0 (photoreal, editorial), then run a Nano Banana Pro edit with the references to make the device exact [16].
  3. Animate approved stills. Use image-to-video (Kling 3.0 or Seedance 2.0; Veo 3.1 when you need sound). Use one camera move per clip and keep clips to 5–10 seconds [17].
  4. Check every frame against the references: groove count and spacing, the black strip layout, no logos, hands and fingers.
  5. Export and name files as listed, then upload: theme slots in the editor, product images in Products › Media.
Copy-ready prompts. The file alumnus/docs/Higgsfield-Prompts-Copy-Ready.txt has every prompt below with the device description and house style already filled in, so you can paste straight into Higgsfield.
Model names change fast. Higgsfield adds models monthly. If a newer version of the same model is in the picker (Nano Banana, Soul, Kling, Seedance), use the newest. The prompts work across them.
